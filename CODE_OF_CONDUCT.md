# Community conduct

We want Webhook Lens to be useful to people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives.

In issues, pull requests, and discussions:

- Be respectful and focus criticism on the work.
- Explain the problem with concrete examples and give others room to learn.
- Respect people's privacy. Do not post personal information, credentials, or private payloads.
- Avoid harassment, discriminatory language, threats, and unwanted promotional messages.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close a conversation, or restrict participation to keep the project constructive and safe. Decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and its impact.

To report a problem, use GitHub's reporting tools. For a concern involving private information, do not repeat that information in a public issue. Ask a maintainer for a private contact route if needed.

This policy applies to project-managed spaces. It does not authorize anyone to speak on behalf of another participant or organization.
